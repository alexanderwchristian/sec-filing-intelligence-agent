Detection workflow

Retrieve two Form 10-K filings from EDGAR, extract Item 1A, segment text, align passages one to one, classify wording differences, and present exact evidence with filing links.

Optional LLM layer

Ask a model to interpret candidate changes only after deterministic extraction. Reject annotations whose evidence quotes are not exact excerpts. Keep AI commentary visibly separate from source facts.

Trust & failure modes

Unusual HTML, repeated boilerplate, major reordering and paraphrases can cause misses or false positives. Extraction fails explicitly rather than producing unsupported findings.

Roadmap

Build a human-labeled public filing benchmark, compare semantic matchers, track reviewer agreement and latency, and introduce source-section anchors.

Product principles

Grounded by design

Auditable

Verbatim source evidence is the contract. The reviewer can inspect the original disclosure instead of relying on generated summaries.

Explicitly limited

The MVP is narrow by choice: Form 10-K risk factors, not investment recommendations or verified material event detection.
